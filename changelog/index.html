1.1.5

2026 年 10 月October 2026

名片辨識精準度全面改進，支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置改用裝置端 AI。Much more accurate card recognition, with on-device AI on devices that support Apple Intelligence.

  • 名片辨識更準確：姓名、公司、職稱的判斷全面改進，名片背面的公司名、拆成兩行的品牌名與地址也能正確辨識More accurate card recognition: better detection of names, companies, and job titles, including company names on the back of a card and names or addresses split across two lines
  • 在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，會使用裝置端 AI 進一步提升辨識準確度，資料一樣不離開你的手機On devices that support Apple Intelligence, on-device AI improves recognition even further, and your data still never leaves your device
  • 日文名片辨識大幅改善，假名的姓名與職稱不再漏掉Much better recognition of Japanese business cards, including names and titles written in kana
  • 設定頁新增「名片辨識」，可查看 Apple Intelligence 的使用狀態New Card Recognition row in Settings shows whether Apple Intelligence is in use

1.1.4

2026 年 9 月September 2026

名片典藏、長按多選，以及看得到的 iCloud 同步狀態。Archive cards, long-press to multi-select, and visible iCloud sync status.

  • 新增名片典藏：過時或暫時用不到的名片，左滑即可典藏，不必刪除；可在「設定」>「已典藏」查看或還原New: Archive cards you no longer need—swipe left to archive instead of deleting. View or restore them in Settings > Archived
  • 長按名片即可進入多選，一次整理、收藏、典藏或匯出多張名片Long-press a card to start selecting, then favorite, archive, or export several cards at once
  • 設定頁新增「同步狀態」，可隨時查看 iCloud 同步是否正常New Sync Status in Settings shows whether iCloud sync is working
  • 意見回饋信會自動附上診斷資訊，方便更快找出問題Send Feedback now includes diagnostic info to help troubleshoot faster

1.1

2026 年 9 月September 2026

自訂你的名片樣式，並推出 CardVault Pro。Customize your card, and introducing CardVault Pro.

  • 「我的名片」新增卡面樣式自訂：可挑樣式、字體、配色、大頭照與排版，做出屬於你的名片風格"My Card" now supports customization: choose a style, font, colors, photo, and layout to build your own look
  • 全新分享合成頁：一鍵把名片做成適合社群的圖片，可調外圍背景與比例，輕鬆發到貼文或限時動態New share composer: turn your card into a social-ready image in one tap, with adjustable background and aspect ratio
  • 新增 CardVault Pro：一次買斷解鎖無限名片張數（免費版最多 15 張）Introducing CardVault Pro: a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited cards (the free plan holds up to 15)
  • 備份匯入支援 vCard 與 CSVBackup import now supports vCard and CSV
