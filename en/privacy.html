PRIVACY POLICY

Your business card data
stays on your device

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") explains how CardVault (the "App") handles your data.

The App is built around one core principle: the business cards you scan stay on your own device. The App requires no account and operates no developer server. By default it sends no network requests at all; only when you actively turn on "iCloud Sync" is your data synced to your personal iCloud (Apple account) — kept in your own private cloud space, which the developer cannot access.

No Account Required No sign-up or login is needed; the developer holds no account data tied to you
Fully Offline Recognition Text recognition and field parsing run entirely on-device, with no network connection required
Your Data, Your Control Cards live on your iPhone by default; with sync on, they stay in your own iCloud — the developer can't read them
No Tracking, No Analytics No third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services are integrated; the developer cannot see how you use the App

1User Identity

Using the App does not require registration, an email address, or logging into any account. The App does not generate, collect, or store any data that could identify you personally.

2Storage of Business Card Data

Every card you scan or import — including the recognized name, company, title, phone number, email, website, address, notes, and the original front and back images — is stored in your iPhone's local database by default. If you enable "iCloud Sync," a copy is also kept in your personal private iCloud database so it can sync across your devices (see Section 8).

  • This data is never uploaded to the developer's or any third party's servers.
  • The developer operates no backend system or database and has no way to access your card data.
  • When you delete the App from your device, this data is removed along with it.

3Camera and Photo Permissions

To provide card scanning, the App requests the following system permissions when needed:

  • Camera: to photograph and scan physical business cards.
  • Photos: to import existing card photos from your library, and to save your "My Card" share image back to your library.

All images are processed and stored only on your device and are never transmitted externally.

4Text Recognition (OCR)

Card text recognition uses Apple's built-in Vision framework and runs entirely offline, on-device; your card images are never sent to the cloud or any external service for recognition. Field classification (identifying names, phone numbers, and so on) is handled by the App's built-in rule-based parser, which likewise requires no network connection and does not send content to any AI service.

5Exporting to Contacts

The App requests Contacts access only when you actively choose "Add to iPhone Contacts," and it writes only the single card you selected at that time. The App never reads, collects, or uploads your existing contacts. Whether and what to export is entirely your decision.

6System Search (Spotlight)

To let you find cards quickly through iPhone's system search, the App indexes card data with the system's Spotlight service. This index exists only on your device, is managed by iOS, and is never transmitted externally. You can disable the App's "Siri & Search" permission at any time in system Settings.

7Sharing and Backup

The App provides share images, QR codes, and data backup features. These are always triggered by you and handed off through iOS's standard share sheet to a destination you choose (Messages, AirDrop, Files, and so on). What you share and with whom is entirely up to you.

8Network Connections and Third Parties

The App connects to no developer backend server and integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not use cookies. Its only network use is the optional "iCloud Sync": when enabled, the App uses Apple's iCloud service to sync your card data to the private iCloud database of your personal Apple account. That data resides in your own iCloud space, is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and cannot be read or accessed by the developer. This feature is off by default and can be turned on or off at any time in the App's settings.

When you tap a phone number, address, or LinkedIn link on a card, your device opens the corresponding system app (Phone, Maps, or a browser). That interaction is between you and that service — the App does not participate in or record it.

9Diagnostic Data

The App does not proactively collect usage data or diagnostic logs. If you enable "Share With App Developers" analytics in iOS Settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports containing no personally identifiable information, used solely to fix issues. This mechanism is managed at the Apple system level, and you may disable it at any time in Settings.

10Children's Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11Future Features

Should the App later introduce other features that require network access (such as an optional AI-assisted parsing mode), those features will — like iCloud Sync — always be opt-in, with a clear explanation of how your data would be handled before you enable them, and this Policy will be updated accordingly.

12Changes to This Policy

This Policy will be revised as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed of our current privacy practices.
