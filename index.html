Features

從一張名片，到整本人脈。From one card to your whole network.

標籤、收藏、典藏Tags, favorites, archive

我的名片 · QR 交換My Card · QR exchange

卡面樣式全免費Card styles, all free

經典、漸層、花紋、玻璃四種樣式，字體、配色、大頭照與排版隨你調；一鍵做成社群分享圖，可調背景與比例。Classic, Gradient, Pattern and Glass styles, plus your own font, colors, photo and layout. Turn it into a social-ready image with adjustable background and ratio.

iCloud 同步 · 無需帳號iCloud sync · No account

通訊錄與匯出Contacts & export

常見問題

Questions

掃描名片需要連網路嗎？Does scanning need an internet connection?
不需要。文字辨識與欄位判斷全部在你的裝置上離線完成，飛航模式也能用。在支援 Apple Intelligence 的裝置上，會改用裝置端 AI 判斷姓名、公司與職稱，同樣不連網、資料不離開手機。No. Text recognition and field detection run entirely offline on your device — even in Airplane Mode. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, on-device AI identifies names, companies and titles; it doesn't go online either, and your data never leaves your phone.
需要註冊帳號嗎？Do I need an account?
不用。沒有帳號、不必填 Email，下載打開就能掃。想在 iPhone 與 iPad 之間同步，開啟 iCloud 同步即可，用的是你自己的 Apple ID。No. There's no account and no email to fill in — just download and scan. To sync between your iPhone and iPad, turn on iCloud sync; it uses your own Apple ID.
我的名片資料存在哪裡？Where is my card data stored?
預設只存在你的裝置上。開啟 iCloud 同步後，會另存一份到你自己的私人 iCloud——仍然不是開發者的伺服器，開發者無法讀取。詳見隱私權政策。On your device only, by default. With iCloud sync on, a copy is kept in your own private iCloud — still not a developer server, and the developer can't read it. See the Privacy Policy.
